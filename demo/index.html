BEHIND THE STORY

A real model.
A little imagination.

The words come from our preserved language model built around a fruit-fly connectome. This page replays recorded inference, with each prediction paired with the neural state that produced it.

The body
The body and its movement are an illustration. The language model does not control locomotion. The NeuroMechFly mesh represents a female fly; the connectome is male. The brain overlay is an illustrative registration between specimens.
The voice
Optional narration uses your browser’s speech synthesis. Playback remains paced by the recorded token frames; voice timing may differ.
